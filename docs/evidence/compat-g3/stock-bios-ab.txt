Interpretation and limits
  This same-ROM/same-device xemu A/B supports the guest-side FS/GS cache repair
  as the mechanism behind the ELF-parse boundary. It is stronger than the
  earlier recreated-ROM replay, but still uses a modified ISO for the passing
  run. The authoritative pinned ISO continues to fail ELF parsing in xemu; do
  not call this acceptance, pinned-image handoff validation, a full BIOS-to-
  kernel acceptance pass, an SMP comparison, or a PERF P4 result. The original two shell/failure runs did not capture the entry handoff; a
  separate candidate run with the same ROM/device setup now does. Its kernel-
  entry RDI and structural block check are recorded in
  `stock-bios-candidate-entry.txt`. This still does not validate the pinned
  image's handoff. The 128 KiB `bios.bin` here is not QEMU's separate 256 KiB
  `bios-256k.bin` used in the initial reference captures (both are from the
  Debian package set; hashes are recorded separately).
